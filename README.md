# Macroeconomic Time-Series Forecasting: Bank Deposit Growth

An Excel-based macro-financial modelling project focused on time-series analysis and forecasting in the context of bank deposit growth.

Bank deposit dynamics can be influenced by macroeconomic conditions, interest rates, inflation, income, liquidity preferences, and financial-market behaviour. A forecasting framework provides a structured way to examine historical patterns and relationships in available data.

## Repository Contents

- `Macroeconomic Time-Series Forecasting project.xlsx` — spreadsheet analysis and forecasting model.

## Project Focus

The workbook contains the project's data, calculations, and forecasting framework. Refer to it for the exact dependent variable, explanatory series, sample period, forecast specification, and outputs.

A typical macro-financial forecasting workflow can include time-series organisation and visual inspection, trend and growth-rate analysis, relationships between deposit growth and macroeconomic variables, forecast construction, comparison of fitted and observed values, and forecast-error assessment.

## How to Use

1. Download and open the workbook in Microsoft Excel.
2. Review source data, variable definitions, and sample dates.
3. Inspect forecasting calculations and assumptions.
4. Identify designated input and scenario cells.
5. Recalculate to evaluate alternative assumptions.

## Macroeconomic Considerations

Time-series forecasting requires attention to stationarity, structural breaks, lag selection, multicollinearity, data revisions, and forecast-horizon choice. Relationships observed historically may not remain stable across monetary-policy or economic regimes.

The model's explanatory relationships should not be interpreted as causal without an appropriate identification strategy.
